AskTheMove is built to answer three questions:

  1. What did I miss?

    The threat, the loose piece or the square you didn’t check, pointed out on the board.

  2. Why is the engine’s move better?

    The reason in a few sentences, taken from lines that were actually calculated.

  3. What if I play my move instead?

    Play it out as a branch, see the strongest reply, then come back to the game.

Evidence first. Then the explanation. Then your own question.

It’s probably for you if

  • You open the engine after a loss and the best move still doesn’t make sense.
  • You would rather understand one mistake properly than skim through ten.
  • You play online or over the board and train on your own time.

It isn’t built for

  • Help during a live game. It analyses finished games, positions you set up, and practice games against its own computer opponents.
  • Memorising opening theory. Other tools do that well.

How much does it cost?

Nothing during the beta. Pricing after the beta isn’t decided yet. You’ll hear about it well before anything costs money, and nothing is charged unless you choose to pay.

When does the beta start?

Invites go out in small groups once the tutor is ready for real games. There’s no fixed date yet. The waitlist is how you’ll hear first.

What devices does it work on?

It runs in a web browser on a desktop, laptop or phone. There’s nothing to install.

Where do the explanations come from?

Stockfish, a free and open-source chess engine, searches the position. A chess rules library checks legality, material and every line that is shown. In the beta, Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, writes the explanation from that evidence. Before you see it, the moves it cites, its numbers and any claim to win material or deliver mate are checked against that evidence. The tutor is built to explain only what the evidence supports.

Can I use it with games from other sites?

Yes. Paste the PGN of any finished game. Most chess sites can export one.

Can I use it during a game?

No. It analyses finished games, positions you set up, and practice games against its own computer opponents. It won’t help you in a live game against another person.

What happens to my email?

It’s stored in a Cloudflare D1 database and used only to send beta news. It isn’t sold or shared. Ask for it to be deleted at any time. The privacy notice has the details.
