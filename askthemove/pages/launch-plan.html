Why ChessLab wins

  • Real, specific pain. Adult improvers (roughly 800 to 1600 rated) run game review, see "best move: Nf5", and still don't know why. Engines give answers; nobody gives reasons you can question.
  • The Claude fit is the product. Your own design rule is that the engine produces verified evidence and the language model only explains it. That is a clean, honest use of the Claude API and an easy story for reviewers.
  • It already runs. Bot play, clocks, game review, saved variations, PGN import/export, accounts, lessons and puzzles all exist with tests. The Claude tutor is the next layer, which is exactly what the $1,000 credit pays for.
  • Big market. Online chess has hundreds of millions of players, and improvers already pay for coaching and premium review.

Fix before you go public

  • The repo describes the goal as "Chess.com parity" and mirrors their bot roster. Anthropic may list your company publicly, so position it as your own product, keep only original bot names and art, and never mention Chess.com on the site.
  • The name "ChessLab" is taken on .com, .ai, .app, .dev and .co, so pick a new brand (below).

Draft description (edit the brand name)
AskTheMove is a chess coach for adult improvers that explains why a move was a mistake, not just which move the engine prefers. Stockfish and a chess rules library produce verified evidence (legal lines, material, threats), and the Claude API turns that evidence into plain explanations the learner can question at any branch of their own game. Bot play, game review and saved variations already work; we are now building the Claude-powered conversational tutor.

Step by step

  1. Done: the brand is AskTheMove on askthemove.online.
  2. Done: askthemove.online is registered on Cloudflare (WHOIS privacy included). Click the registrant verification email Cloudflare sends, or ICANN puts the domain on hold.
  3. Sign up for Zoho Mail → Forever Free, add the domain, and paste Zoho's TXT verification, MX, SPF and DKIM records into Cloudflare DNS. Create prax@askthemove.online (or your name) and hello@.
  4. Publish a one-page site on Cloudflare Pages (free) at the domain: the product line, a screenshot of the review board, the "explains why" promise, and a contact address. No Chess.com mentions.
  5. Wait until mail works both ways (send a test to your Gmail and reply to it).
  6. Create a Claude Console account with the company email. Make sure the right organization is selected.
  7. Go to claude.com/programs/startups → Apply now. Paste the description. Keep every number truthful: no invented users or funding.
  8. When approved: claim the Team year (check seats and amount due at checkout), claim the $1,000 API credit in the org your app uses, and set a spend limit.
  9. Set two reminders: credit expiry (6 months after grant) and Team renewal (a few weeks before 12 months).

Terms to know before submitting

  • Publicity: Anthropic may name you and show your logo as a member.
  • Credits expire 6 months after they're granted, have no cash value and can't be transferred.
  • First-party API only. Your transcription pipeline and other repos use AWS Bedrock; credits won't work there. Point ChessLab at the Anthropic API directly.
  • Residuals: describe the product clearly but keep anything you consider a trade secret out of the form.
  • Tax: you decide whether credits create a tax obligation in India.
  • Partner offers each run on the partner's own terms; redeem only what you'll use.

Praxmodoro is a native macOS focus companion for people who struggle to start tasks, including many adults with ADHD. We are adding an opt-in Claude API feature that turns a stuck, vague task into one concrete first step and suggests the next step after each check-in.
