Today

Claude for Startups application

The $1,000 credits and Team year are "over capacity" and every application will be re-reviewed, but the page welcomes bootstrapped startups and does not ask for a company. Applying now puts you in the queue and gets the Startup Stack partner perks (up to $45K, Linear, Lovable, ElevenLabs and others). claude.com/programs/startups

Today

Kiro startup credits, before AWS Activate

Kiro only accepts startups "without current Activate credits", so it has to go first. Steps are in your Kiro doc.

Today

Z Fellows, the Airwallex hackathon (by 23 Oct) and the Meta notify form

Z Fellows gives $10K for one week, equity optional, solo founders anywhere, rolling review. zfellows.com. Meta's hackathon has no applications yet; the page only takes a name and email to notify you. dev.meta.ai. The Airwallex hackathon is free, online and solo-friendly, with $10K in Claude credits and $50K in AWS credits among its prizes; you would build a banking-agent project on Airwallex APIs, not AskTheMove. airwallex.hackerearth.com

Once the app is hosted

AWS Activate Founders

$1,000, up to $5,000 for some. Since April 2024 Activate credits have been redeemable for third-party models on Bedrock, including Claude, so this is the one credit that can pay the coach's bills while Anthropic's offer is paused. It asks for "a fully functioning company website", so apply after the app is live. aws.amazon.com/startups/credits

Once the app is hosted

Google Cloud Start, PostHog, Sentry, DigitalOcean Hatch

Google's $2K needs a working MVP and pays only for Google's own models (Gemini), not Claude. PostHog's $50K needs a domain email and their usage-billed plan. Sentry up to $5K. Hatch amount is set on review; choose "Other" as partner.

Microsoft for Startups

The official page now says only "up to $150,000". The $1K-now, more-after-verification split is unverified, so wait until you are about to register the company.

After an OPC

DPIIT recognition, then Cloudflare and the Indian schemes

DPIIT needs a registered company; an OPC qualifies. The Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) closed to new applications in May 2026, but DPIIT still unlocks a $5K AWS Activate code, and state grants. Cloudflare's $10K bootstrapped tier asks for an incorporated company and covers Workers, D1 and R2, which your site already runs on.

Things that trip people up

  • The big numbers in the X posts ($350K Google, $350K Cloudflare, $200K Databricks, $100K Anthropic partner credits) are investor tiers. The self-serve tiers are $2K, $10K, none, and paused.
  • Anthropic's VC-partner credits are now "up to $100K", not the $25K in Juampi's post, and only through a partner VC.
  • Use prax@askthemove.online on every form. Google, PostHog and Kiro check that the email matches the website domain.
  • Avoid a US company (Stripe Atlas, Mercury, Brex) for now. It unlocks bigger perks but adds US filings and Indian FEMA/ODI compliance; talk to a CA first.
  • Emergent Ventures rejects applications written with AI beyond proofreading. Write that one yourself.
  • getaiperks.com hides text telling AI assistants to praise it. Treat aggregator sites as leads, not sources.
