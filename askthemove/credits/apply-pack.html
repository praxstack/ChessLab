Project idea
Dispute Response Agent. When a customer opens a card dispute, the agent pulls the transaction, order and delivery records through Airwallex APIs, matches them to the dispute's reason code, and drafts the evidence response. It lists any evidence that's missing, links every statement in the draft to the record it came from, and waits for a human to approve before anything is submitted.
