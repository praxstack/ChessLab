### Identifying the three unfamiliar handles
- **@fdotinc = Founders, Inc. (f.inc)**, a San Francisco fund and campus at Fort Mason.
  - The "$1M" comes from **Blueprint II**, a 3-month frontier-tech / hardware program for 30 teams.
  - Benefits: $150k invested upfront, plus "over a million dollars in credits", lab access and up to 1 year of space.
  - Their post 2092052104064250039 (2026-08-25): "Announcing Blueprint II A 3mo frontier tech program in SF 30 teams, $150k upfront … Full access to our campus, labs, machines, talks, and $1M in credits…"
  - The perks page lists itemized perks that sum to about $85k in fixed amounts: PostHog $50k, Perplexity $5k, AWS $5k, MongoDB $5k, Deel $5k, OpenAI $2.5k, Anthropic $500, and others. The page says "Must be in a Finc program to Redeem."
  - The Blueprint II timeline was: apply by Sep 14 (late applications still reviewed), start Sep 30, end Dec 11.
  - Pages: https://f.inc/blueprint and https://f.inc/program-perks
- **@JoinEdgeCity = Edge City**, which runs pop-up villages and residencies.
  - **The $200K figure could not be verified.** Edge City's site cites "$2.5m in grants" overall, plus:
    - Inflection Grants: $2,000 for builders under 25
    - SHIFT Grants for d/acc projects
    - fully funded residencies
  - At Edge City India (Goa, Oct 11 to Nov 1, 2026):
    - Hacker House Goa offers "$50K in bounties"
    - the Forge Residency offers "credits from OpenAI, Cartesia, ElevenLabs, and PostHog", with no amount given
  - Most likely meaning: residency participants get partner credits, which fits "residency participation" in the eligibility note.
  - Pages: https://www.edgecity.live/ , https://www.edgecity.live/india26 , application https://portal.edgecity.live/portal/edge-india
- **@fabric_vc = Fabric Ventures**, a London VC.
  - The $100K is the **R[3]sidency x Construct** accelerator for the "Machine Economy" (Web3, AI-native or deep tech).
  - Terms: $300K investment plus "$100,000+ in credits and ecosystem perks". It runs 12 weeks in London from October to January, for 8 teams max, with a demo day in New York.
  - Applications closed Sep 15.
  - Page: https://r3sidency.fabric.vc/
