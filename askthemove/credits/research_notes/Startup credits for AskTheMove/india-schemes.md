# Indian government and state startup schemes for a solo software founder (AskTheMove), as of 10 Oct 2026

Founder profile: solo, India (state unknown), bootstrapped, no company, no investors. The product is a consumer AI chess-coach web app.
Verdict legend: **NOW** = possible with no company. **AFTER_INC** = needs an entity (type named). **NO** = not a realistic fit. **UNVERIFIED** = the claim couldn't be confirmed on a source in this session.
Method note: msh.meity.gov.in and seedfund.startupindia.gov.in are JS-heavy or proxy-blocked. Much of the status data comes from aggregators (startupgrantsindia, incorpx, startupadvisory, startupflora) and incubator pages, and is flagged as such. ## 1. DPIIT Startup India recognition: entity types, criteria, benefits

### Takeaway
DPIIT recognition is free and open to a **Pvt Ltd (including OPC)**, an LLP, a registered partnership firm, or (since 2026) a cooperative. It is **not** open to a sole proprietorship or an individual. Verdict: **AFTER_INC**. It is the key that unlocks almost every government scheme. The "angel tax" exemption no longer matters, because Section 56(2)(viib) was repealed for everyone.

### Cited Findings
- Eligible entities: "Private Limited Companies incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 - including One Person Companies (OPCs)", plus LLPs and registered partnership firms. A 2026 notification adds state and multi-state cooperatives. Sole proprietorships are excluded. — [Patron Accounting DPIIT 2026 guide](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide) (secondary)
- Age limit is 10 years from incorporation (unchanged), and **20 years for a new 2026 "Deep Tech" category**. The turnover cap was raised to **₹200 Cr** (from ₹100 Cr), and is ₹300 Cr for Deep Tech. — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide)
- Other 2026 changes: a formal Deep Tech definition, stricter investment restrictions, a revocation clause, and a government power to relax conditions. — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide)
- **80-IAC**: 100% deduction of profits for 3 consecutive years within the first 10. It needs a separate application to the Inter-Ministerial Board after DPIIT recognition, which takes 3–12 months. It applies to Pvt Ltd/LLP. — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide). The incorporation window was **extended to 1 April 2030**. — [StartupAdvisory](https://www.startupadvisory.in/blog-government-funding-schemes-startups.htm)
- **Angel tax**: "Section 56(2)(viib) repealed from FY 2025-26 via Union Budget 2024 for all companies". DPIIT status is no longer needed for this. — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide)
- **Self-certification** under 9 labour laws and 3 environmental laws. **80% patent fee reduction** with fast-track examination. **50% trademark fee reduction**. — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide)
- **CGSS** (Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups) is DPIIT-only. It offers collateral-free debt with guarantee cover up to ₹20 Cr per borrower (85% up to ₹10 Cr, 75% beyond), through banks, NBFCs and venture-debt funds, via jansamarth.in. — [StartupAdvisory](https://www.startupadvisory.in/blog-government-funding-schemes-startups.htm)
- **Fund of Funds (2nd generation)**: a ₹10,000 Cr corpus invested through SEBI AIFs, with a focus on deep tech and AI. It is equity via VCs, not a grant. — [StartupAdvisory](https://www.startupadvisory.in/blog-government-funding-schemes-startups.htm)
- DPIIT recognition is a gate for Zoho for Startups, IndiaAI compute (startup track), GENESIS, CGSS and the SISFS legacy. — prior research [OFF], /mnt/project-files/askthemove/credits/perks-india-grants.md section B

### Inferences
- Section 80-IAC defines an eligible startup as a "company or LLP". An OPC is a company under the Companies Act, so an OPC should qualify for 80-IAC. Patron lists OPCs under Pvt Ltd but does not confirm 80-IAC for OPCs explicitly. **UNVERIFIED**; confirm with a CA.
- For a bootstrapped consumer app with little profit in the early years, 80-IAC has low near-term value. The real value of DPIIT recognition is **access**: state grants, CGSS, incubator seed funds, Zoho, government tenders, IPR fee cuts and self-certification.

### Gaps
- Startup India "partner perks" (AWS/DigitalOcean/other credits through the Startup India portal marketplace): the official page wasn't fetched. **UNVERIFIED** whether any credits are live in 2026.
- The primary DPIIT 2026 notification text wasn't fetched. The 20-year and ₹200 Cr figures come from a secondary source only.

## 2. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS): 2026 status

### Takeaway
**SISFS closed to new startup applications in May 2026**, and no successor has been notified. Verdict: **NO for now**. Watch for a new cycle. Its eligibility rules were DPIIT recognition, incorporation within the last 2 years, and application through an incubator.

### Cited Findings
- Closed to new applications **31 May 2026**. Incubators were to finish selections by 30 Jun 2026. No successor notified. — [StartupAdvisory](https://www.startupadvisory.in/blog-government-funding-schemes-startups.htm); [Saaply: "Closed on May 31st 2026"](https://www.saaply.com/startup-india-seed-fund-opportunities). **Conflicts with** [StartupFlora](https://startupflora.com/blog/sisfs-last-date-to-apply-2026-deadline-extended), which gives the last date as **15 May 2026** and says there is "no confirmed timeline for a new scheme cycle". A Rigzen headline mentions extensions to 31 May or 30 June ([Rigzen](https://rigzen.in/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme-deadline-extended-to-june-30-2026/)). All agree it is **closed by Oct 2026**.
- Former terms: up to ₹20 L grant (PoC/prototype) and up to ₹50 L debt/convertible, disbursed via selected incubators. Required DPIIT recognition, incorporation no more than 2 years before applying, at least 51% Indian promoters, and under ₹10 L prior government support. — [PDEU IIC SISFS page](https://iic.pdeu.ac.in/goi_funding/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme-sisfs/) (via prior research)
- Official portal: https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in/ (JS-only; not readable here).

### Inferences
- Several incubators may still hold unspent SISFS allocations they disburse under their own calls. Ask any incubator you join. **UNVERIFIED**.

### Gaps
- No official DPIIT notice on closure or on a SISFS 2.0 was found. The closing date conflict (15 vs 31 May) is unresolved.

## 3. MeitY (MSH, TIDE 2.0, GENESIS, SAMRIDH, SAMRIDH 2.0) and IndiaAI Mission

### Takeaway
Nothing from MeitY is open to an individual now. GENESIS (DPIIT-recognised Pvt Ltd in a Tier II/III city) closed in Oct 2025. TIDE 2.0 and SAMRIDH run through incubators or accelerators that each have their own windows and need an entity. The **IndiaAI Innovation Challenge 2026** let unregistered teams apply, but it closed on 2 Mar 2026 and its problem statements were government use-cases, not consumer chess. Verdict: **AFTER_INC (Pvt Ltd)**. Watch TIDE 2.0 incubator calls and the next IndiaAI challenge.

### Cited Findings
- **GENESIS** matching investment: up to ₹50 L equity-based, plus 6-month acceleration. Required DPIIT recognition, a Pvt Ltd with at least 51% Indian ownership, registration in a **Tier II/III city**, and a market-ready product. **Applications closed 30 Oct 2025**, with no 2026 round listed. Startups already supported under TIDE 2.0 or SAMRIDH are excluded. — [startupgrantsindia GENESIS](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/meity-genesis-matching-investment-scheme-177) (aggregator)
- **GENESIS Pilot 2.0**: up to ₹40 L for DPIIT startups from Tier 2/3 cities to deploy pilots. Its status is unclear ("closing this month" on an undated listing). — [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/meity-genesis-matching-investment-scheme-177); GENESIS incubator pages: [AIC-ISB](https://aicisb.org/meity-genesis-pilot-funding/), [FTBI NIT Rourkela](https://www.ftbi-nitrkl.org/meity-genesis)
- **SAMRIDH**: the official page is at [msh.meity.gov.in/schemes/samridh](https://msh.meity.gov.in/schemes/samridh) (not readable). Prior research says up to ₹40 L via accelerator cohorts, with the government and the accelerator taking equity, and the main call marked closed. [SEC]
- **TIDE 2.0**: grant via about 51 MeitY incubators, with each centre setting its own call (e.g. [fundsforcompanies listing](https://fundsforcompanies.fundsforngos.org/grant/open-call-for-meity-tide-2-0-india/), [KIIT TBI MeitY page](https://kiitincubator.in/homepage-meity/)). The amount (₹4–7 L) is from an aggregator. — prior research [SEC]
- **IndiaAI Innovation Challenge 2026**: Stage 2 pays ₹25 L each to up to 3 teams per problem. Stage 3 is a 2-year contract of up to ₹1 Cr. Problems: virtual negotiation assistance, intelligent agent mapping, and an AYUSH health system. Eligible: Indian companies (at least 51% Indian), DPIIT startups, and individuals (students, researchers, professionals affiliated with recognised institutions). "Unregistered teams may apply but must register as a company or startup if selected for Stage 2". **Launched 15 Jan 2026; last date 2 Mar 2026 (closed).** — [IndiaAI official](https://www.indiaai.gov.in/article/indiaai-innovation-challenge-2026); [AIKosh PDF](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/manual/010126_Innovation_Challenge_2026.pdf)
- **IndiaAI compute**: the startup track needs DPIIT recognition plus ₹50 L revenue or ₹1 Cr funding. The early-stage track needs 4 years of AI/ML experience and a letter from a senior officer. — prior research [OFF], compute.indiaai.gov.in. Verdict: **NO**. AskTheMove also doesn't need GPUs.

### Inferences
- The IndiaAI Innovation Challenge model (individuals can apply, register only if selected) is the closest MeitY route that needs no company. A 2027 edition is plausible but **UNVERIFIED**. Monitor indiaai.gov.in around Jan 2027.
- GENESIS fits only if the founder lives in or incorporates in a Tier II/III city.

### Gaps
- No 2026 open-call data for TIDE 2.0, SAMRIDH 2.0 or the "Startup Accelerators of MeitY for Product Innovation" (SAMRIDH is that scheme's acronym). The MSH portal couldn't be read.
- IndiaAI Fellowships (for students/PhDs) and IndiaAI Startups Global weren't checked. **UNVERIFIED**.

## 4. DST NIDHI (EIR, PRAYAS), BIRAC, Atal Innovation Mission, MSME

### Takeaway
**NIDHI-EIR is the best central scheme that needs no company**. It pays a fellowship of up to ₹3.6 L over 12 months (₹10–30K/month) to an individual through a host TBI. The catch: you need a science or engineering degree, must be a first-generation entrepreneur with no other income, and must work on the startup full time. Verdict: **NOW (individual)**, through whichever host TBI has an open call. NIDHI-PRAYAS (a prototype grant for individuals) is a second option. **UNVERIFIED**.

### Cited Findings
- **NIDHI-EIR** (PDEU IIC host): up to ₹3.6 L, monthly ₹10K–30K for 12 months. Requires Indian citizenship, a UG/PG degree or diploma in science or engineering, first-generation entrepreneur status with "no prior source of income", full-time commitment, and a business plan. You must not hold more than 10% in another company. Final-year UG students can't apply (you need 4 years of education, or a 3-year degree plus 2 years of work). Apply via a Google form with Aadhaar, PAN, CV, concept note and pitch deck. If selected, you must be incubated at the host TBI. — [PDEU IIC NIDHI-EIR](https://iic.pdeu.ac.in/goi_funding/nidhi-entrepreneurs-in-residence-eir-program/)
- Other EIR host pages: [Ahmedabad Univ Venture Studio](https://ahduni.edu.in/academics/schools-centres/venturestudio/programmes/funding-programmes/nidhi-eir/), [IIM Udaipur](https://nidhieir.iimuic.org), [Bhau Institute COEP (call currently closed)](https://bhau.org/nidhi-eir/), [IIT Patna application PDF](https://iciitp.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Nidhi-EIR-Application.pdf), [India S&T announcement](https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/announcementsopportunity/call-applications-nidhi-eir-department-science-and-technology-dst)
- **IITM Pravartak EIR**: up to ₹4.2 L. Supports a single innovator before incorporation. Focus is cyber-physical systems. Rolling. — [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/entrepreneur-in-residence-eir-iitm-pravartak). The CPS focus makes it a weak fit for a chess app.
- **BIRAC D.E.S.I.G.N. for BioE3**: biotech only. Closes 31 Oct 2026. — [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/design-for-bioe3-challenge-dbt-birac). Verdict: **NO** (sector mismatch). BIRAC BIG is biotech too, so **NO**.
- **MSME debt routes**: CGTMSE gives collateral-free credit up to ₹10 Cr with Udyam registration and no DPIIT requirement. MUDRA Tarun Plus offers up to ₹20 L. — [StartupAdvisory](https://www.startupadvisory.in/blog-government-funding-schemes-startups.htm). These are loans, not grants, and of low relevance.

### Inferences
- NIDHI-EIR's "no prior source of income" and full-time rules mean a founder who is currently employed must quit, or must already be full-time on AskTheMove. Read each host's call.
- Pick a host TBI with a software or AI focus (e.g. IIT/IIM-linked TBIs). The selected EIR is then incubated there, which also opens that incubator's partner perks.

### Gaps
- NIDHI-PRAYAS: reportedly up to ₹10 L for prototypes, to individuals, via PRAYAS centres. It wasn't fetched this session, so it is **UNVERIFIED**. nidhi.dst.gov.in wasn't read.
- Atal Innovation Mission AIC incubators: no 2026 call was fetched. AIC grants go to incubators, and each AIC runs its own pre-incubation (e.g. AIC BAMU, AIC Mahindra University, AIC-Pinnacle, listed below). **UNVERIFIED** amounts.

## 5. State schemes (highlight: funding to individuals before incorporation)

### Takeaway
**Kerala KSUM's IDEA grant** is the clearest state grant that allows applying before incorporation, but it needs Kerala residence or operations, and disbursement needs an entity. **Rajasthan's** policy has a ₹10K/month sustenance allowance for idea-stage innovators, though its status is outdated (UNVERIFIED). **Gujarat SSIP** is for students only. **Karnataka ELEVATE** (up to ₹50 L), **TN TANSEED** (₹10–15 L) and **Maharashtra** grants need an entity registered in that state.

### Cited Findings
- **Karnataka ELEVATE 2026 (25th call)**: up to **₹50 L** one-time grant. Four tracks: General, Shakti (at least 51% women), Unnati (SC/ST) and Aspire (Tier 2/3, formerly Beyond Bengaluru). Opened **25 May 2026**; no deadline stated. — [YourStory Apr 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/04/karnataka-elevate-25th-call-startup-grant-2026); official: https://www.missionstartupkarnataka.org/elevate-karnataka?en. Elevate NXT (deep tech) listing: [incorpx](https://www.incorpx.io/grants/elevate-nxt-2026-deeptech-grant-karnataka-324). Verdict: **AFTER_INC (Pvt Ltd/LLP registered in Karnataka, DPIIT/Startup Karnataka)**. Exact criteria are **UNVERIFIED** and the 2026 window has likely closed.
- **Kerala KSUM IDEA grant**: up to ₹5 L in 2–3 milestone tranches, non-refundable. For founders who "may not yet have a company registered". Requires Kerala residence or primary operations in Kerala. — [rajeshrnair.com blog](https://rajeshrnair.com/blog/software/saas/ksum-kerala-startup-funding-grants-programs-2026.html) (secondary). **Conflicts with** the official KSUM page read in prior research, which said the idea grant is up to **₹3 L**, that innovators can be outside Kerala, and that **incorporation plus a KSUM Unique ID are mandatory for disbursement** ([KSUM Innovation Grant](https://startupmission.kerala.gov.in/schemes/innovation-grant), [OFF] previous session). - **KSUM BCDD grant**: up to ₹10 L for graduates from Backward Classes in Kerala. — [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/bcdd-grant)
- **Tamil Nadu TANSEED 8.0**: ₹10 L (₹15 L for green, rural or women-led). Described as equity-linked (3% stake to StartupTN), though the same listing also says "equity-free". **Closed 30 Jan 2026.** — [incorpx](https://www.incorpx.io/grants/tanseed-8-0-by-startuptn-251); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/?p=91183). Verdict: **AFTER_INC (TN-registered, StartupTN)**; watch for TANSEED 9.0. **UNVERIFIED** criteria.

- **Maharashtra Startup Seed Funding Grant**: amount varies, for idea- and early-stage startups in Maharashtra. Rolling. — [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/maharashtra-startup-seed-grant). **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Uttar Pradesh "The Vault Bharat S2"**: up to ₹1 Cr. Needs a UP office address. Rolling. — [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/the-vault-bharat-season-2)
- **Rajasthan iStart**: ₹10,000/month sustenance allowance for 1 year, "Only for Idea/Prototype Stage Startups". Recommended by a Nodal Institution and approved by the State Level Committee. Pilot-stage marketing support up to ₹10 L (needs 25% angel/VC/incubator funding). ### Inferences
- If the founder is in Kerala, apply to KSUM IDEA now and incorporate on selection. In Rajasthan, ask iStart about the sustenance allowance. ## 7. Cheapest and fastest eligible entity: Pvt Ltd vs OPC vs LLP

### Takeaway
A solo founder **cannot form a standard Pvt Ltd alone**, because it needs at least 2 directors and 2 shareholders. The options are: (a) an **OPC**, which needs one person, is DPIIT-eligible, and is likely 80-IAC eligible (UNVERIFIED); (b) a Pvt Ltd with a family member as the second director or shareholder, which is the best if VC or ESOPs are likely; or (c) an LLP, which needs 2 partners, has lower compliance, and is poor for VC. Typical cost is ₹7–18K all-in through SPICe+, taking 7–15 working days.

### Cited Findings
- Pvt Ltd needs at least 2 directors (up to 15), at least one resident in India, and 2 shareholders. "A single founder can instead register a One Person Company (OPC)." — [TaxAJ 2026 guide](https://www.taxaj.com/learn/?p=288)
- SPICe+ costs about ₹7,000–₹18,000 all-in including professional fees, plus MCA fees and state stamp duty (higher in Maharashtra and Karnataka). Takes 7–15 working days. Ongoing filings: INC-20A, AOC-4, MGT-7, board meetings, ITR. — [TaxAJ](https://www.taxaj.com/learn/?p=288)
- An LLP suits lower compliance and services firms. A Pvt Ltd is better for outside funding and ESOPs. — [TaxAJ](https://www.taxaj.com/learn/?p=288)
- OPCs are explicitly DPIIT-eligible. — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide)
- Razorpay Rize offers low-fee Pvt Ltd/LLP/OPC incorporation. — prior research [OFF], https://razorpay.com/rize/

### Inferences
- **Recommended path**: incorporate an **OPC** (or a Pvt Ltd with a family co-director if available), then apply for DPIIT recognition on startupindia.gov.in (free, typically a few days to weeks). Then register on the state portal. OPC-to-Pvt-Ltd conversion is allowed later when raising money; the 2021 amendments removed the mandatory waiting and threshold rules (from memory, **UNVERIFIED**).
- An OPC cannot take on investors as shareholders without converting. That is fine while bootstrapped.

### Gaps
- OPC-specific fees and compliance (audit is mandatory for all companies, and an OPC has fewer board-meeting requirements) weren't sourced this session. Confirm with a CA.
