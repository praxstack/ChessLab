# Hackathons and build competitions for AskTheMove (solo founder, India), Oct 2026 to Jan 2027

Researched 10 Oct 2026. Profile: solo adult developer in India, bootstrapped, no company yet. AskTheMove is a consumer chess coach built on Claude and Stockfish. X was blocked, so nothing here comes from X. Nothing was registered for.

Legend: NOW = worth entering or registering now. NO = not a fit. UNVERIFIED = a fact not confirmed from a primary page.

## 1. Re-verify the three known leads (Airwallex, Meta, Papermark)

### Takeaway
Airwallex is real and open to India, but it needs an Airwallex-API banking agent, so AskTheMove cannot be entered. Meta's hackathon has no dates or rules yet. Papermark needs a startup that is raising pre-seed or seed, and the founder has no company.

### Cited Findings
- **Airwallex Agentic Banking Hackathon** (HackerEarth). Applications close 23 Oct 2026. The build period runs from 25 Oct, project submissions are due 13 Nov, and Demo Day is 19 Nov in San Francisco, with remote participation optional — [Airwallex](https://airwallex.hackerearth.com)
  - Cash prizes: Founder's Choice $30,000, Judges Choice $20,000, Visa Award $15,000 and Metal Award $15,000 (the last two need Visa or Metal tooling). Credits: Claude $10,000, AWS $50,000 and Base44 $16,000. The page does not say whether credits go to every participant or only to winners — [Airwallex](https://airwallex.hackerearth.com)
  - Eligibility: anywhere in the world, online, age 18+, solo or teams of up to 4, free — [Airwallex](https://airwallex.hackerearth.com)
  - Tracks are all banking agents, such as Treasury Controller, Purchase Agent, Dispute Response, Payroll, Lending, Settlement and Agentic Checkout. Teams "build on Airwallex APIs." Submissions need a demo, a video under 5 minutes and a repo — [Airwallex](https://airwallex.hackerearth.com)
- **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon** (Meta Superintelligence Labs). The page says "Applications opening soon" and offers a notify signup. It is a 10-day virtual event, "worldwide," with a $1M cash prize pool and $150 of Meta Model API credits for every participant. Dates, age limits, team size and themes are not published — [Meta](https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon)
- **Papermark Pitch Competition (Slush 2026)**. Submissions close 19 Nov 2026. Prizes are €10,000, €3,000 and €2,000. Founders from any country can enter, but the startup must be raising pre-seed or seed and have raised $500K or less. One entry per startup. The deck must be a Papermark link. Judging is by AI only (Claude, GPT, Sonar), scoring problem, market, traction, team and the ask — [Papermark](https://www.papermark.com/pitch-deck-competition)

### Inferences
- Airwallex: **NO** for AskTheMove as-is. Every track needs Airwallex banking APIs, so the only way in is a separate fintech-agent build (for example, an Approval-Bound Shopping Agent). That is a new project, not AskTheMove. It is still a real option if the founder wants a side build: solo and India are both allowed. The decision is due by 23 Oct.
- Meta: **NOW (notify list only).** Every participant gets $150 in credits, the event is worldwide, and there are no theme limits yet. AskTheMove would need to use Meta models through the Meta Model API, for example as the coach LLM. UNVERIFIED whether pre-existing projects are allowed.
- Papermark: **NO for now.** The rules talk about "startup" and "company," and say you must be raising. A solo founder with no company and no round fails a strict reading. It could become a yes if AskTheMove is framed as a pre-seed-raising startup, but that is an UNVERIFIED reading of the rules.

### Gaps
- Airwallex: whether the $76K of credits is split among all accepted teams or goes only to winners.
- Meta: dates, rules, and whether existing apps can be entered.

## 2. Other online hackathons with cash or credits, open to India (Oct 2026 to Jan 2027)

### Takeaway
Big-sponsor Devpost hackathons accept solo entrants and existing projects that were "significantly updated." None of their rules name India as excluded. The best fit for AskTheMove is the **PayPal AI Hackathon** (add PayPal payments to the app; due 12 Nov). Qloo and Nebius are possible if AskTheMove is adapted. Lablab's Vultr hackathon gives $200 credits to every participant, but it needs a sandboxed-agent build.
