# Accelerators, fellowships and non-dilutive grants for a solo bootstrapped founder in India (as of 10 Oct 2026)

Founder profile: solo, India-based, bootstrapped, no company, no investors, waitlist only, not a student. Product: AskTheMove, a consumer chess-coach web app (Stockfish + Claude).
Verdict codes: NOW = can apply today as-is; AFTER_INCORPORATING; NEEDS_INVESTOR; NO = ineligible or poor fit. UNVERIFIED = not confirmed on an official page this session.
Sources are official pages fetched on 10 Oct 2026 unless marked. X was blocked; X-derived items come from /mnt/project-files/askthemove/credits/x-posts-grok-report.md.

## Equity-free grants and fellowships: which can the founder apply to now?

### Takeaway
Only four equity-free options fit right now: Z Fellows (rolling, the $10K is optional), Emergent Ventures (India tranche, and the application must not be AI-written), 1517 Medici micro-grants ($1K+, active status unclear) and the Papermark pitch competition (€10K for first place, equity-free, deadline 19 Nov 2026). Most of the rest are age-gated (Thiel, Edge City Inflection and Fellowship), limited to nonprofits (Fast Forward), off-thesis (Rainmatter) or not actually grants (AI Grant is a $250K SAFE).

### Cited Findings
- **Z Fellows**: one-week program, mostly virtual. "You can participate without money or we can give you a $10,000 investment at a $1B valuation cap." Open to founders anywhere, solo OK, no company needed (Z Fellows can help set one up), no age limit, no need to quit a job. Reviewed on a rolling basis, "there is no deadline". Last day is in person in SF or NYC. Verdict **NOW**. The money technically converts to equity at a $1B cap, but the founder can decline it. — [Z Fellows](https://zfellows.com/)
- **Emergent Ventures (Mercatus)**: "a low-overhead fellowship and grant program", open worldwide to applicants aged 13+, with a dedicated tranche for India-focused projects. No equity is mentioned and no amounts or deadlines are stated. "Emergent Ventures no longer accepts AI-written applications" (AI use beyond proofreading is rejected). Verdict **NOW**, but the founder must write it himself. A consumer chess app is a weak fit for EV's "India" theme. — [Mercatus EV](https://www.mercatus.org/emergent-ventures)
- **1517 Fund Medici grants**: "minimum $1k grants to help you validate an idea", aimed at people just getting started, "200+ $1k Grants deployed annually". Apply by writing in (info@1517fund.com). The site's "funds are currently closed" notice refers to the investment funds. Verdict **NOW** (whether grants are currently open is UNVERIFIED). — [1517 Fund](https://www.1517fund.com/)
- **Edge City**: Inflection Grants $2K (under 25), Fellowship (under 25), Edge Frontier Grants "starts at $10,000" with no stated age limit, d/acc grants $5–15K (micro) and $20–40K (development), "More details coming soon". No deadlines are listed; contact grants@edgecity.live. Verdict **NO** for Inflection and the Fellowship unless the founder is under 25. Frontier and d/acc grants are a **weak fit** for a consumer chess app. — [Edge City](https://www.edgecity.live/blog/grantsandfellowships)
- **Thiel Fellowship**: $250,000 grant, no equity mentioned, fellows "skip or stop out of college". The official page does not state an age limit. The historical limit of 22 or under is UNVERIFIED this session (aggregators: [careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-what-is-the-age-limit-to-apply-for-thiel-fellowship)). Verdict **NO** unless the founder is 22 or under. — [Thiel Fellowship](https://thielfellowship.org/)
- **Fast Forward**: for tech nonprofits only. Hard requirements are a nonprofit business model and an existing MVP. $25K+ unrestricted funding. The application window closed 7 Sep, for a Feb–Jun 2027 program. Verdict **NO**. — [Fast Forward](https://www.ffwd.org/accelerator)
- **Rainmatter (Zerodha)**: has committed "$200 million in funding and grants". Themes are Ecology, Human services, Economy, and Pride and identity, with a focus on climate, forests, water, farm and livelihoods. "We fund fellowships and organisations working to restore the environment". Consumer tech is not in scope. Verdict **NO**. — [Rainmatter](https://rainmatter.org/)
- **AI Grant**: not a grant. It invests "$250,000 via a no-cap, no-discount MFN SAFE" plus $350K Azure and partner credits. Single founders can apply. You need a Delaware C-corp if accepted (formable via Stripe Atlas). Batch 4 applications are closed and no next date is given. Verdict: equity, **AFTER_INCORPORATING (US)** when Batch 5 opens. — [AI Grant](https://aigrant.com/)
- **Papermark Pitch Deck Competition**: €10,000 first place, €3,000 runner-up, €2,000 third, described as "for your pre-seed round, equity free". Open to founders from any country and sector who are raising pre-seed or seed and have raised ≤$500K. No traction required. Submissions close **19 Nov 2026**, and winners are announced online and at Slush in Helsinki (18–19 Nov); attendance is not required. Submit the deck as a Papermark link with no password. Verdict **NOW**. The form asks for a company name and round; whether an unincorporated founder qualifies is UNVERIFIED but not excluded. — [Papermark](https://www.papermark.com/pitch-deck-competition)
- **Pioneer**: stopped making new investments in 2024 (prior research). Verdict **NO**. — [prior notes, Pioneer](https://pioneer.app/)
- **Chess-specific**: the FIDE Development Fund goes to member federations. The ISCF Chess in Education grant is for schools and NGOs with existing programs, and its deadline passed on 31 Jul 2026. No Lichess or Chess.com startup grant program was found. Verdict **NO**. — [FIDE/ISCF](https://www.fide.com/iscfs-chess-in-education-grant-program-application-submissions-continue/) (prior research, not re-fetched)
- **Google for Startups Accelerator India**: "equity-free support", but only for "Indian AI-first startups between Seed to Series A". The 2026 class applications closed 19 Apr, with a bootcamp in Bengaluru in late June and Demo Day in October. Verdict **NEEDS_INVESTOR** (Seed stage) for the next cycle, likely spring 2027 (UNVERIFIED). — [Google blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/inviting-ai-first-startups-to-apply-for-google-for-startups-accelerator-india/)
- **SPC Member Residency**: "a six-month, no-cost program" for founders not ready for funding. No dates are listed. Verdict **NOW** (in-person or community expectations are UNVERIFIED). — [SPC F26 page](https://www.southparkcommons.com/news/f26-founder-fellowship)
- **Entrepreneur First (equity-free phase)**: offers an "equity-free grant while ideating" (amount not stated) and accepts people who "might have nothing", with Bangalore listed as a location. — [EF](https://www.joinef.com/). See the equity section for the deadline.

### Inferences
- Realistic equity-free cash in the next 60 days: Z Fellows ($10K, optional), Papermark (€2–10K), a 1517 Medici grant (~$1K), and possibly an EV grant. All four can be submitted without a company.
- An EV application must be human-written. Do not reuse AI-drafted forms from apply-pack.html for EV.

### Gaps
- Mozilla (Builders/Democratic AI grants), Hugging Face and Fireworks grants, Nikhil Kamath's WTF fund and Kunal Shah: not researched this session (tool budget). No known open grant from any of them fits a consumer chess app. UNVERIFIED.
- No grant amounts are published for EV, and no Edge City deadlines are listed.

## Which equity accelerators have deadlines between Oct 2026 and Jan 2027, and does the founder fit?

### Takeaway
The deadlines that matter most: **YC W27 on-time deadline is 2 Nov 2026, 8pm PT**. **EF Bangalore Winter 2027 round-2 deadline is also 2 Nov 2026**, and EF is the best India-based, pre-company, solo-OK fit. **a16z speedrun SR008** has a priority window of 12 Oct–1 Nov per X (UNVERIFIED), and the program is in person in SF from late Jan 2027. **Techstars Anywhere closes 18 Nov 2026** but is restricted to North American time zones. YC, speedrun, SPC, PearX and EF all accept solo, pre-company, idea-stage founders. Indian rolling options: 100X.VC (needs an Indian entity before closing), Peak XV Surge and Antler India.

### Cited Findings
- **Y Combinator W27**: the batch runs January to March in San Francisco. "The deadline to apply on-time is November 2 at 8pm PT", with decisions "by December 11", and late applications are still considered. Deal: "$500,000", of which "$125,000... converts into a fixed 7%" and $375K is an uncapped MFN SAFE. Solo founders: "We regularly accept solo founders". Incorporation before applying: "Nope". The application is open to anyone, anywhere, and YC helps with visas and incorporation. "40% of the companies we fund in each batch are just an idea". The batch is in person in SF. Verdict **NOW**. — [YC apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply), [YC deal](https://www.ycombinator.com/deal), [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq)
- **a16z speedrun SR008**: "$500K for 10% upfront in a SAFE" plus $500K in the next round within 18 months (up to $1M), and "$10M+ in credits". Solo founders with a strong execution record are "strongly encouraged to apply". No incorporation needed (they help). Open worldwide. SR008 runs "from late January through April 2027" in SF, and "In-person attendance is required". Idea stage is OK, but the team needs someone who can build the product. The FAQ lists no SR008 deadline. The priority window of **12 Oct–1 Nov 2026** comes from an @speedrun X post (UNVERIFIED on site). Verdict **NOW** (requires SF travel). — [speedrun FAQ](https://speedrun.a16z.com/faq), [speedrun](https://speedrun.a16z.com/); X claim from x-posts-grok-report.md
- **speedrun Alpha Fellowship**: for students and recent grads, in person in SF, US work authorization required, deadline Oct 11. Verdict **NO**. — [x-posts-grok-report.md / speedrun.a16z.com/alpha](https://speedrun.a16z.com/alpha)
- **Techstars Anywhere**: applications opened 24 Aug 2026, with a **final deadline of 18 Nov 2026**. The program starts 8 Mar 2027, with Demo Day 3 Jun 2027. It is remote-first, but "Eligible companies are in North American time zones", with three in-person offsites. Investment terms and solo/incorporation rules are not stated on the page. Standard Techstars terms of $220K ($20K for ~5% common + $200K MFN SAFE) are UNVERIFIED this session. Verdict **NO** for Anywhere (time zone). Techstars London and other city programs may differ (UNVERIFIED). — [Techstars Anywhere](https://www.techstars.com/accelerators/anywhere), [Techstars list](https://www.techstars.com/accelerators)
- **Entrepreneur First, Bangalore Winter 2027**: starts Feb 2027. "Apply by 02 November 2026 to be considered for the second selection round", with three rounds in total. Participants spend 14 weeks in Bangalore, then the final 3 months in SF. Equity-free grant while ideating, then "Up to $250K once you form a company" (equity % not stated). Accepts individuals with no co-founder or idea. EF builds US companies. Verdict **NOW** (best India-based fit). — [EF apply](https://apply.joinef.com/), [EF](https://www.joinef.com/)
- **South Park Commons Founder Fellowship**: "$400K upfront in exchange for 7% on a standard SAFE" plus $600K guaranteed in the next round and up to $1M in credits. Bootcamp at the SF, NYC or **Bangalore** office. Solo applicants accepted, with a "strong bias for applicants who can build and prototype". The F26 deadline (2 Aug 2026) has passed, and no next cycle is listed (the past pattern suggests a spring 2027 cycle, UNVERIFIED). Verdict **NOW** for the next cycle. — [SPC F26](https://www.southparkcommons.com/news/f26-founder-fellowship)
- **Antler India**: "up to ~$425K (~INR 4 crores) for 11% equity". The Residency page says INR 4Cr (~$470K) for 11%, so the two figures differ. Residency in Bengaluru for "pre-launch, pre-product, or pre-revenue" teams, with an AI-first focus. "Next Residency will be announced soon". Direct engagement is possible. The Before Day Zero community welcomes solo founders looking for a co-founder. Verdict **NOW** (reach out directly or join Before Day Zero). — [Antler India](https://www.antler.co/location/india)
- **100X.VC**: "₹1.25cr in each startup" via iSAFE "for 15% of Future Equity". Rolling applications, open to founders building from India. Deal closure happens once "the Indian entity is incorporated". Solo eligibility is not stated. Verdict **NOW** to apply, **AFTER_INCORPORATING** to close. — [100X.VC apply](https://www.100x.vc/apply)
- **Peak XV Surge**: the application form accepts "Idea/Prototype" and pre-launch stages and asks for the company's country of registration. Terms and cohort timing are not stated, and it responds in 2–3 weeks. A Surge article says applications are open all year. Solo eligibility is unclear. Verdict **NOW** (terms UNVERIFIED). — [Surge apply](https://surge.peakxv.com/apply), [Surge year-round](https://surge.peakxv.com/article/surge-applications-are-now-open-all-year-round)
- **Accel Atoms (AI Cohort 2026 with Google AI Futures Fund)**: co-invests "up to $2M", equity-backed, with up to $350K in Google compute credits. For Indian and Indian-origin founders. "Founders at idea or prototype stage — traction is not mandatory". Requires at least one Gemini integration. The 2026 cohort closed 26 Jan 2026, and later applicants are considered for future cohorts. Verdict **NOW** (would need a Gemini integration; next cohort deadline UNVERIFIED, possibly ~Jan 2027 if the pattern repeats). — [Atoms](https://atoms.accel.com/news/ai-cohort-2026-with-google)
- **PearX**: "$500K and $2M", "target 7% ownership". Must relocate to the SF Bay Area. "Absolutely—we welcome solo founders". Applications accepted year-round, with a winter cohort from January to April. Verdict **NOW** (requires relocation to SF). — [PearX](https://pear.vc/pearx/)
- **Sequoia Arc**: "bi-annual open call for pre-seed and seed stage founders". Terms are company-specific, and no dates or India eligibility are given. Verdict UNVERIFIED. — [Sequoia Arc](https://www.sequoiacap.com/arc/)
- **HF0**: "The residency for repeat founders". The page shows batch starts of Sep 13 and Jan 4, and terms are not stated. Verdict **NO** (first-time founder). — [HF0](https://www.hf0.com/)
- **Lightspeed India Ascends**: a deeptech accelerator program. Verdict **NO** (consumer app, off-thesis; not fetched in detail). — [Venture Intelligence](https://news.ventureintelligence.com/private-equity/lightspeed-launches-%E2%80%98india-ascends%E2%80%99-program-to-back-early-stage-deeptech-startups)
- **f.inc Blueprint**: hardware and frontier tech, SF-based. Verdict **NO** (prior research). — [f.inc](https://www.f.inc/blueprint)

### Inferences
- A single application sprint before **2 Nov 2026** covers YC W27, EF Bangalore and speedrun SR008, since their questions overlap heavily. Z Fellows and Papermark can reuse the same material.
- For a solo founder with no traction, YC, speedrun and SPC will weigh shipped product and a technical track record. Live MVP usage numbers before 2 Nov would materially help.
- India-resident options with no relocation: EF Bangalore (then SF), Antler India, 100X.VC, Surge and Atoms.

### Gaps
- Not fetched this session (tool budget): Neon Fund, Afore (Founders in Residence), 16VC, India Accelerator, Venture Catalysts, Microsoft/NVIDIA Inception (these are credit programs, not accelerators), and Buildspace successors. All UNVERIFIED.
- The SR008 priority window dates are not on the official site. Techstars terms and solo/incorporation rules are not on the page.

## Which pitch competitions with cash are open now?

### Takeaway
Papermark (€10K / €3K / €2K, equity-free, closes 19 Nov 2026, global, no traction required) is the only verified cash competition open now.

### Cited Findings
- See Papermark above. — [Papermark](https://www.papermark.com/pitch-deck-competition)

### Inferences
- Its low bar (a deck link, free plan) makes it worth entering alongside the YC and EF applications.

### Gaps
