### Takeaway
No change since 9 Oct. The $1K API credits and Claude Team offers are still "over capacity". You can still apply in the Console, and acceptance gives Startup Stack perks, office hours and events. VC backing isn't required. Anthropic program credits can't be spent on Bedrock or Vertex.

- FAQ, 10 Oct: "we're currently over capacity on the Claude Team and $1,000 API credit offers". Anthropic will re-review all applications, which "may result in a change in the status of some applications". Those who already claimed the offer keep it. — [claude.com/programs/startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups)

Examples: ElevenLabs (12 months free plus 33M characters), ClickHouse ($5K), Granola, Hex, and Augment Code ($100). Partner-VC route: up to $100K in extra API credits, claimed through the VC. — [claude.com/programs/startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups)
- Credits "can't be used on AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI, or other third-party platforms". Members who get credits also get higher rate limits. — [claude.com/programs/startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups)
- Apply at https://platform.claude.com/offers/startups-application — [claude.com/programs/startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups)
- The expanded program launched on Tue 6 Oct 2026 at SF Tech Week. It offers a free year of Claude Team (up to 5 seats), $1,000 in API credits, Claude Marketplace access, and office hours. Eligibility per TechCrunch: "founded within the last five years, or that received funding within the last two years". — [TechCrunch, 6 Oct 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/anthropic-gives-startups-a-free-year-of-enterprise-service-and-1000-in-token-credits/)

- Separately, Max and Team plans include monthly API credits in the Console. The founder already has these. — prior verified report (x-posts-grok-report.md, POST 8, claude.dev blog)
- **Airwallex Agentic Banking Hackathon**, a newer route to Claude credits: $10,000 in Claude credits, $50K in AWS credits and $16K in Base44 credits in the prize pool. Open to anyone 18+ "anywhere in the world"; solo entry allowed.
