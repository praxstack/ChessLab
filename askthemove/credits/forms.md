## 5. AWS Activate Founders
- URL: https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits
- Account (VERIFIED): an AWS Builder ID (the step guide says personal email; the credits page says professional) plus an Activate profile with a business email matching the domain. You then link an AWS account with admin rights; the credits page says it must be on a "Paid Tier Plan" (card on file).
- Eligibility: self-funded or bootstrapped; new to Activate credits; founded within 10 years; pre-Series B; "fully functioning company website" (REPORTED/VERIFIED on the AWS step-by-step guide).
- Credits: $1,000, with select participants getting up to $5,000.
- Fields (VERIFIED from the official AWS guide, not the live form): credit tier; business info (product, target market, current traction); funding stage; most recent funding date; AWS account link and verify. No edits after submit. Decision in 5 to 10 business days.
- Conflict: getting Activate credits now blocks Kiro credits, so apply to Kiro FIRST.
- Personally: the AWS account with a card, and the Builder ID.
