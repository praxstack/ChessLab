# Ship the app, then incorporate an OPC

As of 10 October 2026, a solo founder in India with no company and no investors can already apply to about forty programs. The highest-value ones are the **Claude for Startups application** (its $1K API credit and Claude Team offers are paused as "over capacity", but Startup Stack perks worth up to $45K still flow) ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/programs/startups)), **Kiro and then AWS Activate Founders ($1K to $5K, the only reachable credits that can pay for Claude itself, via Bedrock)** ([AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits)), and the equity accelerators that take solo founders with no company: **YC W27 (on-time deadline 2 Nov 2026)** and **Entrepreneur First Bangalore (round-2 deadline 2 Nov 2026)** ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply), [EF](https://apply.joinef.com/)). The nearest hard dates are **23 Oct** (Airwallex hackathon applications close, but it needs a separate banking-agent build, not AskTheMove), **30 Oct** (the Qloo and Nebius hackathons), **2 Nov** (YC and EF), **12 Nov** (PayPal AI Hackathon, the best hackathon fit for AskTheMove) and **19 Nov** (Papermark pitch competition). Hosting the app unlocks Google Cloud Start ($2K), DigitalOcean Hatch and a Mixpanel free year, and gives every application a real product to point to. Incorporating a **One Person Company (OPC)**, the only company form one person can set up alone, unlocks DPIIT recognition, Cloudflare for Startups ($10K, the best match for the current Cloudflare stack), NVIDIA Inception, xAI, the business-verified Microsoft credits, and almost every Indian government scheme. The headline Indian scheme, **SISFS, closed to new applications in May 2026**, so the only central money open to an individual right now is the **NIDHI-EIR fellowship (up to ₹3.6 L)**. OpenAI, Google's $200K+ tiers, GitHub, Vercel, AWS Portfolio and most inference providers need an investor or partner referral, and incorporating does not change that.

## Twelve corrections to the 9 October research

Several points in the earlier files (ai-credits.md, cloud-credits.md, perks-india-grants.md, x-posts-grok-report.md) are now wrong or weaker than first reported. Where they conflict, today's notes win. First, the AWS post saying Activate credits can be spent on third-party Bedrock models, including Anthropic's, is dated **2 April 2024, not May 2026**. It is an old policy, not new news ([AWS Startups blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/startups/aws-activate-credits-now-accepted-for-third-party-models-on-amazon-bedrock/)). Second, the Microsoft for Startups split ($1K right away, $5K after business verification within 90 days) is **unverified**. The official page now says only "up to $150,000", and the one detailed source is a Microsoft Q&A answer marked "AI-generated content may be incorrect" ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups), [MS Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/a/12780061)). Third, **SISFS is closed**. Aggregators agree it stopped taking new applications in May 2026 and no successor has been announced, though they disagree on the exact date (15 or 31 May) ([StartupAdvisory](https://www.startupadvisory.in/blog-government-funding-schemes-startups.htm), [StartupFlora](https://startupflora.com/blog/sisfs-last-date-to-apply-2026-deadline-extended)). Fourth, **a solo founder cannot form a standard Pvt Ltd**, which needs at least two directors and two shareholders. "A single founder can instead register a One Person Company (OPC)" ([TaxAJ](https://www.taxaj.com/learn/?p=288)), and OPCs are explicitly DPIIT-eligible ([Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide)). The earlier advice to "incorporate a Pvt Ltd/LLP" should be read as OPC, or as a Pvt Ltd with a family member as the second director. An LLP also needs two partners.

The other corrections matter less but still change decisions. The earlier advice to choose Kiro or AWS Activate was too strict: AWS now says "Start with Kiro Startup Credits, then grow into Activate Credits", so take Kiro first and apply to Activate after ([AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits)). The Airwallex hackathon was listed in the AI-credits notes as a route to Claude credits, but every track requires building a banking agent on Airwallex APIs. AskTheMove cannot be entered, and only a separate side project could compete ([Airwallex](https://airwallex.hackerearth.com)). The Papermark pitch competition is borderline rather than a clean yes. It is equity-free and open to any country, but it is written for a startup that is "raising pre-seed or seed", one entry per startup ([Papermark](https://www.papermark.com/pitch-deck-competition)). Cloudflare Tier 3 has risen from $5K to **$10K**, but its rules still say "incorporated" ([Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/)). Amazon's $150 AWS participation credit has run out ([Amazon rules](https://amazonappdev2026.devpost.com/rules)). Kerala's KSUM idea grant is **₹3 L** on the official page, not ₹5 L, and needs incorporation before any money is paid ([KSUM](https://startupmission.kerala.gov.in/schemes/innovation-grant)). DPIIT's turnover cap is now ₹200 Cr, and the angel-tax exemption no longer matters because Section 56(2)(viib) was repealed for all companies ([Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide)). Finally, the X-post extras in x-posts-grok-report.md labelled PostHog, Notion, Algolia, MongoDB and DigitalOcean as AFTER_INCORPORATING. The official pages do not ask for a company, so all of these are NOW.

## Hosting the app turns four "maybes" into approvals

Some programs open their doors today but ask for a working product, or start a clock you don't want running before launch. Google Cloud Start is for startups "with a working MVP", founded within 24 months, and planning to seek venture funding. It requires a billing account on your domain. Its $2K lasts 12 months and comes with a free year of Workspace Business Plus ([Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/startup/pre-seed), [FAQ](https://cloud.google.com/startup/faq)). Apply on the day you deploy. Mixpanel's free year requires data flowing within 90 days, and Postmark's $75 is for bootstrapped founders who are already charging.

## An OPC plus DPIIT unlocks the second tier

Incorporation is the dividing line for Cloudflare, NVIDIA, xAI and all government money. Cloudflare Tier 3 gives $10K for "Bootstrapped or self-funded" startups, but its rules also say "Your company was incorporated no more than 10 years ago", and only first-time applicants are accepted. A rejection now could use up your one chance, so wait for the entity ([Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/)). NVIDIA Inception requires members to "be officially incorporated" ([NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/)). xAI's terms define the applicant as "the company or other organization on whose behalf an application is submitted" ([xAI](https://x.ai/legal/startup-program-terms)). DPIIT recognition is free, rolling and open to a Pvt Ltd, an OPC, an LLP or a registered partnership, but not to a sole proprietorship. It is the key to 80-IAC, IPR fee cuts (80% off patents, 50% off trademarks), the CGSS loan guarantee and the state grants ([Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/dpiit-startup-recognition-2026-guide)). SPICe+ incorporation costs about ₹7–18K all-in and takes 7–15 working days ([TaxAJ](https://www.taxaj.com/learn/?p=288)).

Two caveats apply to the OPC choice. An OPC cannot take investors as shareholders without first converting to a Pvt Ltd, which is fine while bootstrapped. Whether an OPC qualifies for 80-IAC is likely, since the section covers a "company", but it is not confirmed in a source. Check both with a CA. If a family member can act as the second director, a Pvt Ltd avoids the conversion step later. A Delaware C-corp through Stripe Atlas (about $500) is a separate decision. It brings US FEMA/ODI obligations and makes sense only if YC, AI Grant or US VCs accept you.

## Conclusion

This research changes the bottleneck. On 9 October the question looked like "which programs need a company". Today the binding gates are a **live product** and **one-shot applications**. A hosted app with real usage numbers before 2 Nov makes YC, EF, speedrun, Google Start and AWS meaningfully more likely to say yes. Cloudflare's first-timers-only rule and Microsoft's reported 90-day verification window both reward waiting until incorporation instead of applying early. The Claude bill itself is barely covered by any program today. Anthropic's $1K is paused, Google's and DigitalOcean's credits exclude Claude, and only AWS Activate through Bedrock pays for it. So the coach's cost plan should assume Max-plan API credits plus AWS, with Gemini Flash as the cheap tier until January.

Several things remain unknown and should be checked before you rely on them: the Microsoft credit split, the speedrun priority dates, whether Papermark accepts an unincorporated entrant, whether Devpost rules allow Indian entrants (India is not named as excluded, but it is not named as allowed either), whether an OPC gets 80-IAC, and when Anthropic's capacity returns. On the Indian side, NIDHI-EIR is the one live central rupee route. It suits a founder who is already full-time and holds a science or engineering degree. Everything else in government funding waits on an OPC and DPIIT recognition.
