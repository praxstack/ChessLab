## Notes and recommendations

1. **Anthropic (the coach's actual model):** the Claude for Startups $1K/Team offers are **over capacity** (verified on claude.com/programs/startups, 2026-10-09). You can still apply in the Console; acceptance gives Startup Stack perks, and you may get credits when capacity returns. The official page says bootstrapped founders are welcome and does not mention incorporation. VC partner credits are now "up to $100K" (not $25K), but only through a partner VC.
2. **Most effective route to Claude credits right now: AWS Activate Founders.** It gives $1K, possibly up to $5K, and since the May 2026 change it can be spent on **Claude via Amazon Bedrock**. You need a functioning website, so host the app (even a beta) before applying. You also need a paid-tier AWS account with a card. Moving from the Anthropic API to Bedrock means swapping the SDK client (`AnthropicBedrock`), and model IDs differ.
3. **Google Start tier ($2K)** covers only Google's own models, so it pays for Gemini, not Claude. It is worth it if you add Gemini Flash as a cheaper model for simple explanations. Requirements: founded within 24 months, MVP live, prax@askthemove.online matching the site.
4. **Microsoft:** the $1K non-investor credit is reachable; the extra $4K needs business verification, so do that after registering. Azure OpenAI coverage is likely but not confirmed on the page.
5. **Incorporation unlocks:** Cloudflare Tier 3 ($10K, $2.5K Workers AI), NVIDIA Inception, xAI Grok for Startups, Fireworks, and Microsoft's full $5K. Registering a Pvt Ltd or an LLP, and getting DPIIT recognition for Indian programs, expands options significantly. A sole proprietorship may not count as "incorporated" for Cloudflare or NVIDIA.
6. **Conflict between kiro and AWS Activate:** the Kiro page says startups enrolled in AWS Activate don't qualify for Kiro credits. Choose: Activate is more valuable for inference.
7. Aggregator discrepancies: llmperks lists DeepInfra at 1B tokens, but the official page says about 50B. Llmperks also says Claude for Startups requires equity funding and a 4-year age limit, but the official page says bootstrapped founders are OK. The freetokens GitHub repo only points to its live site (freetokens.custats.info); it has no inline list.
