# Frontend design skills: research (8 Oct 2026)

**Window:** 8 Sep – 8 Oct 2026. **What worked:** web search, Hacker News (Algolia), GitHub pages and skills.sh install counts. **What failed:** Reddit, X and YouTube (blocked or no keys). Growth compares a snapshot from 28 Aug–6 Sep with today.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **Taste Skill** ([Leonxlnx/taste-skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill)). Stars went from 65.8k to 92.6k, and installs from 275K to 577K, the fastest growth of any design skill. hashp (1 Oct) names it the pick for landing pages. It has variance, motion and density dials, plus a calm "soft" variant. Weakness: it only works if the model follows the prose. Install: `npx skills add https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill`
2. **Impeccable** ([pbakaus/impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable)). Stars went from 48.4k to 78.4k, and installs from 200K to 318K. Version 4.0 picks a direction from 188 design "worlds." It adds a 59-rule slop detector and an edit hook, and neither needs an LLM. An HN commenter recommended it on 16 Sep. Install: `npx impeccable install`
3. **Emil Kowalski skills** ([emilkowalski/skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills)). Stars went from 19k to 44.2k, and installs from 151K to 332K. These are motion skills: animate, animation-vocabulary, review-animations and apple-design. Install: `npx skills@latest add emilkowalski/skills`
4. **Hallmark** ([Nutlope/hallmark](https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark)). It has 25.5k stars (about 3.5k in July) and 60K installs. It ships 21 themes, about 57 slop gates and `study <URL>`, which turns a reference site into a design.md. Nobody mentioned it this window, and an HN commenter in July said its results all look alike. Install: `npx skills add nutlope/hallmark`
5. **Anthropic frontend-design**. This is the baseline, with 962K installs (#7 on skills.sh). Muzli (28 Sep) called it "the best short brief on AI taste." An HN commenter (10 Sep) said it "loves an all-caps mono caption." Install: `claude plugin install frontend-design@claude-plugins-official`
6. **UI/UX Pro Max** ([nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill)). It has the most stars (133.9k) but the slowest install growth (+40%), and it sits at #261 on trending. Superdesign called it "a library, not a librarian." Use it for palettes, fonts and accessibility. Install: `/plugin marketplace add nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill`

Skills 4–6 are already loaded here.

**Anything newer?** Nothing launched this window beats them. The real change is that Taste and Impeccable now outgrow Pro Max and Hallmark. The new skills got almost no attention (deslop-skills, Boxy: 1 HN point each).

## What to use

**AskTheMove.** Use Taste (variance at about 7, motion at about 6) for an editorial, off-grid layout. Use Emil's animate for annotations that draw themselves in (arrows, circles, underlines), then review-animations to cut extras. Run Hallmark `study` on the references below, and use the Impeccable detector as the final check.

**Praxmodoro.** Use Taste `high-end-visual-design` (motion at about 2, density at about 3). Add Impeccable `quieter`, Emil `apple-design` for gentle, Mac-native easing, and Pro Max for a calm palette and contrast checks. Keep one focal action and no busy autoplay.
