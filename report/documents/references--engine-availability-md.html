Engine availability — 7 September 2026

Nine configurations are installed and selectable: Stockfish 19, 18 and 16; Maia3 79M, 23M and 5M; Maia2 rapid and blitz; and Lc0 0.32.1 with a T1-256x10 distilled network. These are six named engine/version choices and three additional model variants, not nine unrelated engine families. The isolated runtime occupies approximately 1.9 GB. See installation hashes and direct move receipts and authenticated game API verification.

Chess.com's official Torch announcement documents Torch on its analysis page. That is not an authorized standalone native UCI distribution. None was located or installed. Torch Human, Torch 4 and Lite labels observed in the user's recording remain references, not local integrations.

The Komodo website retained a notice ending sales on 31 May 2026 and scheduling the site through 31 July 2026. A Dragon 1 Free link was found, but direct HTTPS downloads from its endpoint failed because the certificate had expired. Certificate validation was not bypassed; no Dragon binary or third-party substitute was installed. No current Dragon 3.3 license or download was acquired.

The downloaded binaries and neural weights stay in ignored server storage. Upstream license identities are recorded with sources; no proprietary license or permission to redistribute Chess.com art is asserted. This record distinguishes current local availability from vendor offerings and historical research.

Stockfish Lite in the recorded menu was not separately reproduced. The server uses the named full native releases above, not Chess.com's browser-packaged engine builds.
