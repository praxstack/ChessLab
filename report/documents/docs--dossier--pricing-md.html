What the first pricing experiment could be

These are proposed price tests, not approved plans or promises of available features.

Stage or audience Candidate offer What I would learn
Research participants Free, explicitly time-limited prototype Can users finish the learning journey and return?
India individual pilot Test ₹199 versus ₹299/month Does a focused review tool earn incremental spending alongside existing tools?
International individual pilot Test $6 versus $9/month Is the improvement in understanding worth paying for, and are costs sustainable?
Coach pilot, later Test a small paid workspace after student usage exists Will coaches pay for saved preparation time and repeat student work?

Do not describe different currency offers as conversions of one another. They are separate market experiments. Start monthly, keep cancellation simple, disclose usage limits and avoid a lifetime plan. Annual discounts can wait until retention and support costs are understood.

The source report proposed free local analysis, Pro at $9.99/month or $79–89/year, Coach at $19–39/month, and academy seats at $3–8/student/month. Those are broader, untested packaging ideas. In particular, “unlimited” cloud conversations are risky before usage tails and cost per explanation are measured.

Costs the research understates if read too quickly

A language-model response is only one cost. Include repeated explanation attempts, deeper engine analysis, storage, support, failed payments, refunds, monitoring, educator review, and customer acquisition. Client-side computation can reduce hosting costs while increasing device compatibility and performance work.

The supplied example of 10,000 learners × 10 positions × two explanations × $0.01 is $2,000/month for model inference alone. At $0.03 it becomes $6,000. Neither per-call amount is a vendor quote. A paid subscriber may also subsidize free users, so model both cohorts.

My pricing opinion

I would keep manual exploration and exports useful without payment, then charge for a clearly demonstrated improvement in tutoring or follow-up practice. I would not charge merely to expose an ordinary engine evaluation that users can get free elsewhere. Before promoting any price, show the learner a working lesson and ask them to make a real choice.
