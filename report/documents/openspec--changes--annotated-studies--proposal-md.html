Portable annotated studies

Baseline 6795f52 protects 82 application tests and 60 recorded flows. The full local platform/tutor objective and original games, branch identity, accounts, review, collections and progress remain protected. This product increment does not alter governing gates. Baseline source/manifests are retained rollback pointers.

The official analysis and PGN guides describe comments, annotations, variations and export (https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8648793-how-can-i-add-comments-and-variations-to-games and https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8705305-how-do-i-get-a-pgn-of-my-game, read 2026-09-13). Current import/export discards alternatives and comments. Preserve them, add position-bound comments and move glyphs, and provide a versioned local study file for exact ChessLab tree/notes/navigation round trips. PGN remains the interoperability format; its text grammar cannot encode every arbitrary learner note and application identity.

Graph: observed loss -> frozen portable-study contract -> failing round-trip/legal/ownership checks -> reuse the installed chess.js parser and rules -> position annotation and import/export controls -> recorded original and new flows -> root review/video PR -> backed-up exact local installation. The installed parser is pinned at chess.js 1.4.0 and its AST is covered by tests; no second chess library is needed. One implementation plus one evidence-driven repair cycle; stop after two no-gain candidates. Human multiplayer and billing keep their existing order. Local free-form model coaching remains later work, not a claim made by annotations.
