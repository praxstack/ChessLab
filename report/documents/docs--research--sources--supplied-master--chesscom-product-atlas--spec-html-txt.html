<section class="hero"><div class="eyebrow">Interaction and system specification</div><h1>Stateful surfaces behind the visible product.</h1><p>This specification decomposes the observed product into bounded services and state machines. It does not claim Chess.com uses this architecture.</p></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Logical domains</h2><div class="grid grid-3">
<div class="card"><h3>Identity & entitlement</h3><p>Account creation, provider login, activation, profile, restrictions, plan state and usage allowances.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Game platform</h3><p>Matchmaking, challenges, clocks, legal state, ratings, Daily deadlines, bots, tournaments and spectator sessions.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Improvement platform</h3><p>Puzzle selection/scoring, lessons/courses, engine jobs, review artifacts, Insights jobs and training progress.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Community</h3><p>Friends, messages, clubs, forums, chat, moderation and notifications.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Content & events</h3><p>News, streams, event metadata, broadcasts, schedules, standings and game feeds.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Commerce</h3><p>Plan catalog, localized price, trial, checkout, platform billing, family invitations and course purchase rights.</p></div>
</div></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Key state machines</h2><div class="grid grid-2">
<div class="card"><h3>Live game</h3><p><code>setup → searching → matched → active → ended → review_available</code></p><p>Cancellation, disconnect, timeout, resignation, draw and checkmate are distinct transitions.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Game Review</h3><p><code>not_generated → queued → processing → highlights → move_review</code></p><p>Error, entitlement limit and deeper cloud-analysis paths must remain separate.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Rated puzzle</h3><p><code>served → attempting → correct|incorrect|hinted → complete → next|limit</code></p><p>Rush, Battle and Daily hearts use separate scoring and termination rules.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Membership</h3><p><code>basic → trial → paid → renewing|cancelled → expired</code></p><p>Family membership adds invitation states and secondary-account eligibility.</p></div>
</div></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Canonical entities</h2><div class="table-wrap"><table><thead><tr><th>Entity</th><th>Minimum fields</th><th>Notes</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td>User</td><td>id, username, activation, locale, timezone, restrictions, preferences</td><td>Social availability must not be inferred only from authentication.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Entitlement</td><td>plan, trial, renewal, feature allowances, course licenses</td><td>Course purchase rights are separate from plan tier.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Game</td><td>players, ruleset, time control, moves, clocks, result, rating deltas</td><td>Daily and Live require different deadline models.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Review</td><td>game id, engine configuration, accuracy, classifications, graph, explanations</td><td>Fast post-game summary may differ from full review.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Puzzle attempt</td><td>puzzle id, mode, moves, hints, result, rating/points, elapsed</td><td>Daily hearts and Rush/Battle strikes are mode-specific.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Learning progress</td><td>content id, completion, streak, rank, review schedule</td><td>Lessons, courses, puzzles and endgames use different progress semantics.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Community object</td><td>club/thread/message/member role/moderation state</td><td>Activation and policy restrictions gate actions.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Event</td><td>metadata, rounds, games, results, standings, schedule, streams</td><td>Broadcast ingestion and presentation are separable.</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div></section>

<section class="section"><h2>Non-functional requirements</h2><div class="card"><ul><li>Server-authoritative clocks and move legality for rated human games.</li><li>Idempotent move submission and reconnect-safe game state.</li><li>Accessible keyboard navigation and non-color-only move/review labels.</li><li>Strict separation between active-human-game assistance and post-game analysis.</li><li>Regionalized commerce with tax disclosure and platform-billing reconciliation.</li><li>Moderation, abuse reporting, rate limits and audit trails for community actions.</li><li>Cross-device preference synchronization with intentional mobile capability gaps documented.</li></ul></div></section>
