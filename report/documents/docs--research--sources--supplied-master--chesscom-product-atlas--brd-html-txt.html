<section class="hero"><div class="eyebrow">Business requirements document</div><h1>Chess.com public product surface, mapped without pretending.</h1><p>The business requirement is a traceable parity dossier that explains the observable Chess.com experience and the evidence confidence behind every statement. It is intended for product, design, engineering and QA teams considering a clean-room chess platform or conducting competitor analysis.</p></section>
<section class="grid grid-3"><div class="card"><h3>Decision</h3><p>Use capability parity as the baseline, but do not copy branding, proprietary assets, text or inaccessible implementation.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Primary user</h3><p>A cross-functional team that needs a grounded product map rather than a mood board.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Success condition</h3><p>Each requirement links to a screen, public evidence and a PASS/PARKED/FAIL decision.</p></div></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Business objectives</h2><div class="card"><ol><li>Document the acquisition-to-retention loop: signup → first game → review → training → social/event return.</li><li>Separate free utility from paid improvement features.</li><li>Expose cross-platform deltas before implementation planning.</li><li>Keep legal, safety and evidence boundaries visible to decision-makers.</li></ol></div></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Product flywheel</h2><div class="grid grid-3"><div class="card"><h3>Play</h3><p>Live, Daily, Bots, Coach, variants and tournaments generate games and identity.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Understand</h3><p>Game Review, Self Analysis, Accuracy, Insights and opening stats turn games into feedback.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Practice & return</h3><p>Puzzles, lessons, courses, Practice, Endgames, social ties, events and streaks create reasons to return.</p></div></div></section>

<section class="section"><h2>Visual-system evidence boundary</h2><div class="grid grid-2"><div class="card"><h3>What is documented</h3><p>Chess.com exposes board-and-piece customization, backgrounds, animation, sounds, interface dark mode and accessibility-oriented high-contrast options. Those capabilities are mapped to first-party settings evidence.</p><p class="source">Evidence: <a href="https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8615285-how-do-i-manage-my-settings" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">S39</a> <a href="https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8594320-how-do-i-change-my-background-board-and-pieces" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">S40</a> <a href="https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8704531-how-do-i-turn-off-sound" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">S41</a></p></div><div class="card"><h3>What remains PARKED</h3><p>Exact production colors, font files, type scale, spacing grid, radius/elevation tokens, default board/piece chrome and desktop/mobile visual deltas were not measurable because current rendered pages could not be captured.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Mock visual language</h3><p>The package uses an independent charcoal, green and cream visual system with system fonts and Unicode chess pieces. These values support legible documentation and are not presented as extracted Chess.com design tokens.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Replacement rule</h3><p>Future approved captures may replace PARKED visual rows only when desktop, mobile and interaction-state screenshots plus computed-style measurements are retained as evidence.</p></div></div></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Commercial structure</h2><div class="grid grid-2"><div class="card"><h3>Basic</h3><p>Unlimited core chess and community entry, with bounded review/training allowances.</p><p class="source">Evidence: <a href="https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8562418-what-does-each-level-of-premium-membership-get-me" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">S58</a></p></div><div class="card"><h3>Premium ladder</h3><p>Gold concentrates learning and ad removal; Platinum adds unlimited analysis; Diamond adds explanations, Insights and advanced stats; Family shares Diamond across six accounts.</p><p class="source">Evidence: <a href="https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8562418-what-does-each-level-of-premium-membership-get-me" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">S58</a> <a href="https://www.chess.com/family" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">S62</a></p></div></div></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Constraints and risks</h2><div class="grid grid-2"><div class="card"><h3>Evidence risk</h3><p>JavaScript-heavy and authenticated UI could not be visually captured. Placement, exact responsive geometry, current paywall modal designs and current price amounts remain PARKED.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Trademark and copying risk</h3><p>This package uses an independent label and original mock UI. It does not reproduce Chess.com logos, artwork, exact copy, private data or source code.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Product complexity</h3><p>Real-time play, rating systems, engine analysis, content, social moderation, subscriptions and broadcast tooling are distinct systems with very different operational risk.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Fair-play boundary</h3><p>Live-assistance internals are out of scope. A future product should prevent analysis from helping active human games.</p></div></div></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Acceptance criteria</h2><div class="card"><ul><li>All required HTML artifacts exist and cross-link.</li><li>Every feature row has location, trigger, behavior, states, entitlement, source and screen mapping.</li><li>Unknown facts are PARKED.</li><li>Six mock routes render at desktop and mobile widths without horizontal overflow.</li><li>Keyboard focus reaches primary controls, and local mock pages emit no console errors.</li></ul></div></section>
