Investor memo / Research dossier

What would change my mind?

An angel-investor view of the opportunity, the missing evidence and the next useful milestone.

Would I invest today?

On the evidence in this folder, I would not yet underwrite a venture investment. I would support a tightly bounded validation effort. That is an analytical viewpoint, not a funding offer. The problem is understandable and the founder has a concrete experience to investigate. The record does not yet show a product, customer interviews, learning results, retention, paying users or a repeatable acquisition channel.

A beautiful dossier, installed skills and detailed specifications improve communication and preparation. They do not reduce the largest commercial risk: whether learners will repeatedly choose and pay for this experience.

The case in favor

Chess gives the team deterministic rules and strong engines. That makes illegal continuations and many factual errors easier to catch than in an unconstrained tutor. The motivating confusion is specific enough to demonstrate quickly. The product can also complement existing playing platforms instead of needing its own matchmaking network.

A good teaching interaction could serve individual improvers, coaches and clubs. However, each audience has a different purchase decision. I would require evidence from one before funding simultaneous consumer, academy and API businesses.

What an angel would want to see completed

The following is my proposed diligence checklist. It is not a set of user-approved product gates, nor a claim that every investor uses these thresholds.

Question Evidence I would ask for Current record
Is this a recurring problem? Real completed games and interviews showing repeated confusion with current review tools One motivating conversation; no interview corpus
Does the product work? A live local or hosted walkthrough from import through nested branching, comparison, return, reload and export Draft specification only
Is the explanation trustworthy? Educator-reviewed positions, legal replay, documented failures and unsupported-answer handling Architecture recommendation, no product corpus
Does it teach? Predictions before reveal, independent teach-back and unseen related positions after a delay No learning study
Will people return? Cohorts using it after subsequent games, with full denominators and dates No usage cohorts
Will people pay? Actual transactions or a concrete paid pilot, including cancellations and refunds No payment evidence
Can the business reach users? A tested channel with conversion, acquisition effort/cost and repeatability Proposed coach/club route
Can it be operated? Measured serving/support costs, data export/deletion, license plan and reliability evidence No deployed system
Can the team deliver? Shipping history relevant to this product, a named chess educator and a focused next milestone Tooling prepared; team/adviser commitments not established
Are ownership and funding clear? Entity/cap-table/IP records, founder runway and use of proceeds when fundraising becomes concrete Not provided in this record

Completion comes in stages

Validation-ready means a small prototype is usable in an observed session and its limitations are stated. It need not have subscriptions, native apps or cloud accounts.

Pilot-ready means unrelated target learners can complete the loop with acceptable support, preserve their work, and understand what the tutor can and cannot claim. A chess educator has reviewed the examples and failure cases.

Investment-ready means the founder can connect a working product to learning value, repeated use, willingness to pay and a plausible channel. No software checklist can substitute for those observations. A company itself is never “complete”; financing should fund the next uncertainty worth resolving.

The first diligence experiment I would propose

Recruit a modest, deliberately selected group of adult improvers and at least one qualified chess educator. Ten to twenty learners could expose basic usability failures; that sample would not establish a general learning effect. Use their actual completed games where permitted, plus matched related positions.

Compare the proposed interaction with the learner's normal review workflow. Balance presentation order and difficulty. Record predictions first, then teach-back, then delayed transfer. Observe whether the learner returns after another game. Offer a concrete paid continuation only after the prototype produces value.

Agree in advance on the sample, rubric, thresholds, budget and stopping point before this becomes an execution plan. The numeric suggestions here are exploratory and must not silently become governing gates. If users enjoy the conversation but cannot apply the concept later, revise the teaching interaction. If they learn but do not return or pay, reconsider packaging or audience.

YC and the larger story

YC's current requests include adaptive tutoring for young children's reading, writing and arithmetic. Chess has conceptual overlap with individualized teaching, but it is not that specific request. Theme alignment is not acceptance or investment validation. YC Requests for Startups.

I would pitch the observed learning problem and the founder's evidence before a generalized “counterfactual tutoring platform.” Chess's legal state and checkable sequences do not automatically transfer to other subjects. Expansion is a second research hypothesis, not a multiplier to add to today's market size.

Open source or a startup?

An open-source local study and branching core could be useful on its own. It can invite educators and contributors to inspect behavior and contribute reproducible examples. A commercial service might later charge for hosted tutoring, synchronization or coach workflows. Contributors, stars and downloads would still not establish a paid business.

Choose ownership and licensing deliberately. Stockfish is GPLv3 and its distribution obligations apply to the exact build shipped; separating it into a worker does not by itself settle the overall licensing analysis. Stockfish licensing explanation. No application license has been selected in ChessLab.

The funding conversation I would avoid

The source report's $120K–$300K remote six-month MVP and $450K–$900K US-heavy estimate describe a much broader team and release than the first proposed experiment. They are planning estimates, not quotes or a justified fundraise. Do not raise or spend against them without an explicit scope, staffed plan, runway and milestone budget.

My present recommendation is to spend the next effort on a legal position corpus and one observable teaching interaction. That is the shortest route to evidence that could change an investor's mind.
