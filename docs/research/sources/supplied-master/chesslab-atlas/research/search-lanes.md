# Research discovery log

The research used bounded, first-party discovery across these question families:

1. Legal terms, automated access, AI use, copying, API boundaries and intellectual-property warnings.
2. Human play, time controls, matchmaking, challenges, board interaction, reconnect and post-game flows.
3. Daily chess, conditional moves, vacation, tournaments and variants.
4. Bot catalogs, adaptive opponents, fixed engine practice and guided Coach play.
5. Game Review, self-analysis, cloud analysis, charts, annotations, import, export and continuation.
6. Game Collections, opening explorer, opening statistics, personal archives and bulk actions.
7. Rated puzzles, Puzzle Rush, Puzzle Battle, Daily Puzzle, custom modes and puzzle history.
8. Lessons, learning paths, opening practice, master games, thematic drills and endgames.
9. Profiles, ratings, Insights, advanced statistics, achievements and progress history.
10. Broadcasts, ChessTV, events, streamers, spectating, news and public embeds.
11. Friends, direct chat, clubs, forums, comments, blogs, notifications and safe-mode behavior.
12. Settings, privacy, accessibility, localization, mobile applications and web/mobile feature differences.
13. Fair play, cheating definitions, reporting, blocking, moderation, appeals and protected-user controls.
14. Memberships, entitlements, family plans, gifts, trials, billing and premium analysis limits.
15. Public API, platform operations, observability, backups, privacy export and deletion.
16. User-owned ChessLab context, current implementation evidence and known verification gaps.

A focused follow-up wave resolved source gaps for platform differences, fair-play boundaries, product entitlements and current user-facing workflow details. Research stopped when all 18 domains had first-party evidence and each of the 180 capability rows resolved to at least one source and one unique ChessLab acceptance contract.
