<section class="hero"><div class="eyebrow">Interaction and system specification</div><h1>Stateful surfaces behind the visible product.</h1><p>This specification decomposes the observed product into bounded services and state machines. It does not claim Chess.com uses this architecture.</p></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Logical domains</h2><div class="grid grid-3">
<div class="card"><h3>Identity & entitlement</h3><p>Account creation, provider login, activation, profile, restrictions, plan state and usage allowances.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Game platform</h3><p>Matchmaking, challenges, clocks, legal state, ratings, Daily deadlines, bots, tournaments and spectator sessions.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Improvement platform</h3><p>Puzzle selection/scoring, lessons/courses, engine jobs, review artifacts, Insights jobs and training progress.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Community</h3><p>Friends, messages, clubs, forums, chat, moderation and notifications.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Content & events</h3><p>News, streams, event metadata, broadcasts, schedules, standings and game feeds.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Commerce</h3><p>Plan catalog, localized price, trial, checkout, platform billing, family invitations and course purchase rights.</p></div>
</div></section>
<section class="section"><h2>Key state machines</h2><div class="grid grid-2">
<div class="card"><h3>Live game</h3><p><code>setup → searching → matched → active → ended → review_available</code></p><p>Cancellation, disconnect, timeout, resignation, draw and checkmate are distinct transitions.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Game Review</h3><p><code>not_generated → queued → processing → highlights → move_review</code></p><p>Error, entitlement limit and deeper cloud-analysis paths must remain separate.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Rated puzzle</h3><p><code>served → attempting → correct|incorrect|hinted → complete → next|limit</code></p><p>Rush, Battle and Daily hearts use separate scoring and termination rules.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Membership</h3><p><code>basic → trial → paid → renewing|cancelled → expired</code></p><p>Family membership adds invitation states and secondary-account eligibility.</p></div>
</div></section>
