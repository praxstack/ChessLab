Return without losing your work

A proposed part of the ChessLab learning experience.

What this screen must make possible

A clear return-to-game state: large actual-game board restored; a non-blocking message Back at your original position; two saved branches visible in a compact study drawer. Resume AI game or Continue review primary action. Previously asked questions are attached to branches. No discard dialog needed to return.

Review note

Saved branches stay visible after returning. A real return must restore the exact original node and its history. Show Resume AI game or Continue review according to the session type, not both indiscriminately.
