Recover without losing the study

A proposed part of the ChessLab learning experience.

What this screen must make possible

A landscape UX state sheet with THREE clearly separated readable panels. Panel one Invalid PGN at move 12, edit input and retry; existing study preserved. Panel two Coach unavailable, continue exploring the board and retry explanation. Panel three Could not save, keep study open with Export a copy. No false success or lost-work reassurance without evidence.

Review note

These are three separate states, not three errors that must appear together. Preserve the full existing study on invalid import. Local save failure is the first-build concern; the image mentions cloud save, which remains a future option.
